# Revised SEO Publishing Roadmap

## Batch 1: Publishable With Current Data

Publish the previously approved planet mantra and planet remedy pages. Add separate Shani Beej and Shukra Beej mantra pages because they have high demand and sufficiently different SERPs. Add one career astrology landing page that explains the relevant planets and embeds or directly links into the existing personalized remedy workflow. Do not describe it as a career prediction unless the product is extended to provide one.

## Batch 2: Highest-Value Data Expansion

Build reviewed source data for Ketu in the eighth house, Rahu Mahadasha, Shani Mahadasha, and Ketu Mahadasha. These are the strongest opportunities that the current generic planet remedy data cannot fully satisfy. The application already computes Dasha, making Dasha pages a natural product extension.

## Batch 3: Product Feature Expansion

Create deterministic foreign-settlement and foreign-travel rules using reviewed Jyotish sources. Then build separate educational and calculator pages. Foreign settlement and its calculator have distinct SERPs, while `abroad settlement astrology` should be consolidated into the main educational page.

## Batch 4: Reusable Programmatic Systems

Only after expert-reviewed data exists, create structured models for planet-in-house placements and planet conjunctions. Start with the proven queries rather than generating every permutation. Ketu in the eighth house should be first, followed by Ketu and Rahu in the seventh house.

## Consolidation Decisions

- Career astrology and job astrology belong on one page because their top-result domain overlap is 0.636.
- Career and job calculator phrases belong together because their overlap is 1.000.
- Rahu mantra, Rahu Beej mantra, and Rahu mantra jaap belong on one comprehensive page because overlap is strong.
- Shani Beej mantra can support a separate page after editorial review because it has 9,900 monthly searches and lower overlap with the broad mantra query.
- Shukra Beej mantra can support a separate page because it has 8,100 monthly searches and low overlap with the broad Shukra query.
- Surya and Budh Beej terms should begin as substantial sections on their parent mantra pages. Separate them later only if Search Console shows the parent cannot rank for those queries.

## Publication Rule

No page should be published merely because a keyword has volume. It must either be fully supported by current structured data or have its missing data and product functionality completed first.
