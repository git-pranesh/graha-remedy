# Koala Brief For Approved Pages

Use one primary keyword per article from `koala-approved-keywords.txt`. Secondary keywords in `content-plan.csv` belong on the same page and should not become separate articles.

## Mantra Pages

Answer the query immediately with the approved mantra from `data/remedies/remedies.json`. Include the planet's Sanskrit and English names, transliteration, plain-English purpose in Vedic tradition, exact repetition and timing from the source data, weekday, preparation, a step-by-step practice, common mistakes, related fasting and donation practices, and a link to the personalized remedy calculator.

Do not claim guaranteed outcomes. Do not invent a mantra translation, pronunciation, scripture, number of repetitions, timing, or benefit. Do not write a separate article for every spelling variation listed as a secondary keyword.

## Planet Remedy Pages

Explain signs traditionally associated with a weak or afflicted planet, then present the approved mantras, fasting, donations, puja steps, deity, and temple guidance from `data/remedies/remedies.json`. Distinguish traditional belief from proven fact. Include a short section explaining why a full birth chart matters and link to the personalized remedy calculator.

## Vedic Remedies Hub

Explain what graha remedies are, when they are traditionally used, and the major remedy types. Give a concise section for all nine planets and link to each mantra and planet-remedy page. Explain that remedies are selected from the full chart rather than from a single symptom.

## Evil Eye Page

Frame evil-eye and negative-energy concepts as cultural and spiritual beliefs. Use only the Rahu, Ketu, Mars, and Saturn practices supported by the application's mapping and remedy data. Do not intensify fear, diagnose supernatural harm, or discourage medical or mental-health support.

## Required Editorial Rules

- Target India in English, but explain Sanskrit terms for readers worldwide.
- Use an introduction, descriptive sections, practical steps, key takeaways, and a concise FAQ.
- Write for the reader, not for a fixed word count. Approximately 900-1,300 words is sufficient when the topic warrants it.
- Mention the primary keyword naturally in the title, introduction, and one heading. Do not stuff it.
- Do not mention Koala, AI, SEO, keywords, competitor pages, or these instructions.
- Do not reproduce competitor wording or unsupported claims from SERP research.
- End with the free personalized remedy CTA.
