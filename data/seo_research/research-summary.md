# SEO Research Summary

## Decision

Do not generate the original 115 or 342 planet/problem articles. Launch with the 15 pages in `content-plan.csv`, review their indexing and impressions, and expand only from observed demand.

## Evidence

- 182 intended seed phrases were measured for India in English.
- Only 26 had entries in DataForSEO's keyword database.
- The verbose planet/problem phrases generally had no measurable volume.
- Google Keyword Planner expansion returned 4,938 suggestions.
- A strict relevance pass retained 1,373 candidates and selected 171 across 39 clusters for validation.
- Exact metrics were available for 159 candidates.
- Live top-10 India SERPs were inspected for 25 representative queries.

## Strongest Findings

- Planet mantra searches have the strongest relevant demand: Shani, Rahu, Surya, Shukra, Budh, Brihaspati, Chandra, Ketu, and Mangal.
- Rahu and Ketu remedy pages have meaningful demand and relatively weak mixed SERPs.
- A Vedic astrology remedies hub has 1,900 monthly searches and is necessary for internal linking.
- Moon and Mercury remedy pages have moderate demand and direct support in the structured remedy data.

## Rejected Or Deferred Topics

- `venus remedies` is dominated by the pharmaceutical company Venus Remedies, so its 6,600 volume is unrelated.
- `career astrology` and `job astrology` mostly return career-prediction tools. The current product provides remedies, not a dedicated career prediction, so those queries are deferred.
- `foreign settlement astrology` and its calculator variant expect settlement predictions and chart yogas that the current product does not provide.
- `vastu remedies` primarily expects home-layout corrections and products not contained in the current remedy catalog.
- Specific house, conjunction, Mahadasha, and Lal Kitab pages are deferred because the current static source data is not detailed enough to support them accurately.
- Spelling, language, Beej, Gayatri, and jaap variants should be consolidated into the relevant planet mantra page rather than published as separate pages.

## SERP Observations

- Most mantra results contain AI overviews, videos, People Also Ask, and Hindi-language publishers even for English targeting in India.
- Several remedy SERPs include Reddit, Quora, Facebook, Scribd, small astrology sites, and commercial gemstone sellers. This indicates an opportunity for a well-sourced, fast, non-commercial reference page.
- The content should include exact practice instructions and strong internal links; generic prose alone will not be differentiated.
